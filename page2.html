Programs

The Casa Program supports children as they shape their personalities and intelligence, exploring Montessori materials through sensory discovery in a prepared environment.

Casa Program (Ages 3–5)

In the Primary Grade School Program, the classroom’s prepared environment aids the intellectual and physical development of students until they achieve independence from adults and gain confidence as they relate to the world.

Primary Grade School (Ages 6–8)

In the Intermediate Grade School Program, the students deepen their knowledge about their universal responsibility to care for life and the environment.

Intermediate Grade School (Ages 9–12)

Our Junior High Program nurtures students, guiding their emotional and social growth while strengthening problem-solving, collaboration, and communication skills to prepare them for independence.

Junior High School (Ages 13–16)

The Senior High School Program further equips the students with the skills and knowledge necessary for future success. The curriculum is GAS Plus, which allows the students flexibility in exploring career options and moving up to the tertiary level of any local or international college or university.
